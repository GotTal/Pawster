# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

Pawster welcomes people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, and focus disagreements on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sharing someone's private information, and persistent unwanted contact are not welcome.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces. Report conduct concerns privately to GotTal at [xkstudio@icloud.com](mailto:xkstudio@icloud.com); include only information needed to assess the concern. GotTal may request changes, remove content, close discussions or restrict participation according to severity and repeated behavior. Maintainer decisions should explain the relevant conduct and avoid unnecessary disclosure of personal information. No fixed response time is promised.

喵贴欢迎不同背景与经验的参与者。请尊重他人、给出具体建议，讨论分歧时聚焦工作内容。不接受骚扰、歧视、威胁、泄露他人隐私或持续进行不受欢迎的联系。

本准则适用于项目的 Issue、PR、讨论区及其它由项目管理的空间。遇到行为问题，请通过 [xkstudio@icloud.com](mailto:xkstudio@icloud.com) 私下联系 GotTal，仅提供评估所需的信息。维护者可根据严重程度及重复情况要求改正、移除内容、关闭讨论或限制参与，并说明相关行为，避免不必要地披露个人信息。不承诺固定回复时限。
