Design: a cream-white kitten hugging a wallpaper card, with a second lilac card suggesting a collection. Mint background, teal contours, peach cheeks/sun, clear small-size silhouette. No lettering, baked-in outer rounded corners, or external frame.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on November 12, 2026. The generated master is preserved unchanged as `Pawster-Icon-Master.png` (1254 × 1254, opaque RGB). No user wallpaper or third-party cat character was used as an image reference.

The production icon is `App/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-1024.png`: an opaque RGB PNG, mechanically resized to 1024 × 1024 using high-quality bicubic interpolation. Xcode’s single-size iOS AppIcon configuration generates device sizes; the system applies its outer icon mask. The project explicitly includes the asset catalog and selects `AppIcon` with `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_APPICON_NAME`.

The generated artwork is new project artwork. Original third-party code and license notices are unaffected. This record documents its origin and prompt, without claiming trademark clearance or a particular jurisdiction’s copyright status for AI-generated work.

Source-only integration: no IPA build, push, or merge performed. Asset compilation, automatic dark/tinted treatment, and actual SpringBoard display remain to be checked in the next authorized Xcode test build and device installation.

## Generation prompt

Create one finished iOS app icon for Pawster, a cute cat-themed wallpaper installation/management app. Single square 1024x1024 artwork, not a mockup or a grid. A charming cream-white kitten with two pointy ears, tiny deep-teal eyes, a small smiling mouth and subtle peach-pink cheeks peeks over and hugs a wallpaper card with two little rounded paws. The wallpaper card has a simple teal rolling hill and a small peach sun, with just one subtly offset lilac card behind it to suggest wallpaper collection. Clear unified central silhouette, friendly and calm, very readable at small app-icon sizes. Fresh pastel mint background extending completely to all four square corners, cream white main shapes, restrained teal contours, lilac and peach accents. Polished minimalist softly dimensional illustration with clean smooth edges, very gentle shadows, lots of visual clarity, no fine textures or tiny details. Cat and card together occupy about 76 percent of the canvas with balanced safe margins. Full-bleed opaque square background; do NOT round the outer canvas corners or add any icon border, surrounding device, frame, title, letters, logo text, watermark or extra props. Prefer a distinctive cute cat wallpaper symbol over decorative clutter.
